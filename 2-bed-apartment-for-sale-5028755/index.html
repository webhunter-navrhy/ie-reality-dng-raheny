DNG Estate Agents are delighted to present to the market No. 8 The Sycamore, Clonshaugh Woods. This well-presented two-bedroom first-floor apartment is in good condition throughout and offers bright, comfortable and well-proportioned accommodation. The property also benefits from a south-facing rear balcony, providing an excellent outdoor space and plenty of natural light.

The accommodation comprises a spacious open-plan living/kitchen area, two good-sized bedrooms, a south-facing rear balcony, a main family bathroom and ample storage.

No. 8 The Sycamore is sure to appeal to first-time buyers, investors and those looking for a comfortable home in a well-established and convenient development. Clonshaugh Woods is ideally positioned close to a wide range of local amenities, with Northside Shopping Centre and Clarehall Shopping Centre both within easy reach. The villages of Portmarnock and Malahide are also nearby, while the area is well serviced by a choice of bus routes and offers easy access to the M50, M1, Dublin City Centre and Dublin Airport.

With its good condition, bright accommodation and south-facing rear balcony, No. 8 The Sycamore represents an excellent opportunity to acquire a well-located two-bedroom apartment.

Viewing is highly recommended to fully appreciate all this property has to offer.
